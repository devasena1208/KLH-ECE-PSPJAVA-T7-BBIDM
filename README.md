**Abstract:**

The Blood Bank Inventory and Donor Matching System is a software application designed to simplify and automate the management of blood units, donor records, and blood requests in a blood bank. Traditional blood bank management often relies on manual registers, which can be slow, error-prone, and risky when quick decisions are needed in emergencies. This system provides a centralized platform for efficiently managing blood stock and donor information. The system allows staff to add, update, search, and remove blood unit records, maintaining details such as blood group, quantity (units), collection date, and expiry status. It also manages donor registration and maintains donor eligibility and donation history. Users can search for available blood by blood group and quickly match compatible donors when stock is low. The system can track expiry dates and help staff identify blood units nearing expiry, improving safety and reducing wastage. By automating routine operations, the proposed system reduces paperwork, minimizes data-entry errors, saves critical time during emergencies, and improves the reliability of blood stock information. It also provides an organized and quick method for maintaining records and generating stock reports. The Blood Bank Inventory and Donor Matching System can be implemented as a web or desktop application and can be further enhanced with features such as SMS/email alerts for donors, blood group compatibility auto-matching, expiry alerts, and role-based access for hospital staff vs admins. Overall, the system aims to provide a simple, efficient, and life-saving solution for managing blood bank activities.
